There is a cost to being wrong. It keeps most organisations from choosing at all.

Every AI app you allow has to be approved, given a model, fenced with rules and logged, one app at a time. So the answer becomes not yet, and the people who would have moved first wait. Impire does that work once, for the workspace. Whatever connects is held to what you decided, and when you change your mind, the change applies everywhere, the same day.
