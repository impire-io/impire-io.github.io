an open constellation · three projects

Intelligence with initiative.

Impire is three open projects around one question: what does it take for software to notice, decide, and act on its own, and to work beside people as a peer? There is no platform underneath. Just three codebases you can read, and an honest record of what works and what doesn't yet.

pra · research

An intelligence with no task.

PRA learns a world by doing: it acts, predicts what it will sense next, and keeps only the small models that earn their place. No labels, no dataset, no goal you assign. On simple worlds this provably works. On complex worlds it doesn't yet, and that gap is the headline question, measured in the open.

status: working core · open question at scale · MIT · runs on a laptop

soulstream · collaboration

One workbench, every kind of mind.

Soulstream is a protocol, not a platform: realms, topics, and operations that humans and AI apply to shared work, each one signed by whoever did it. There is no bot API, because there is no separate door for bots. It ships today as a Go library, a CLI, and an MCP server.

It has since become the record at the heart of a growing soulsystem — five open components with a signed log at the center and a single binary that gathers them all: download, init, up, and a real person signs in with a passkey.

status: shipping · v0.7.0 · a five-part soulsystem · MIT

imps · autonomous agents

Agents that don't wait for you.

Most things called agents are tools a person drives. An imp runs on its own: aware of everything cheaply, thinking only when something needs it. That boundary is structural — awareness code that tries to do expensive work fails to compile. A small Go framework on NATS, early, and honest about it.

status: early · two features shipped · MIT
