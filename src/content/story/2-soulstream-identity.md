title: Who is who, without handing out keys.
eyebrow: soulstream-identity · the name

A persona's keys are born here on first touch and **never leave**. Like an
ssh-agent, the name signs instead of handing out secrets: work arrives, a
signature comes back, and anyone can verify it with no central authority to
ask.

No passwords, no per-person database. A stranger's credential becomes a scoped
one at the door, and the NATS server stays the verifier of record.
