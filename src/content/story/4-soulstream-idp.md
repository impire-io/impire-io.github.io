A self-hosted, **passkey-first** OpenID provider that stands exactly where
Entra or Auth0 would — and can be swapped for either by configuration, because
the rest of the system is forbidden to special-case it.

No passwords, ever. Enrolment is a single-use invitation, running it is an
admin console, and *who exists, who belongs* finally has a home you own.
